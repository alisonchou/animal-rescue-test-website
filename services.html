Adoption process

How to adopt with Twin Cities Animal Rescue:

  1. Browse our website for available pets.
  2. Submit an inquiry and share information about your home and lifestyle.
  3. Talk with our team about whether you're a good fit.
  4. Prepare your home and welcome your new companion!

Foster program

Not ready to commit yet? Fostering is a great way to spend time with a furry friend for the short-term.

Here's how it works:

  1. Talk with our team about your eligibility and what you're looking for.
  2. Work with our team to identify a foster placement.
  3. Give a pet a temporary home! While fostering the pet, we'd love if you could provide pictures and updates to help the pet find a permanent home.

Volunteer roles

  • Animal care: Care for our animals. This involves tasks from cleaning, grooming, feeding, walking, and playing with the animals.
  • Adoption: Welcome visitors and provide guidance about adoption.
  • Outreach: Host events and help spread the word about our rescue.
