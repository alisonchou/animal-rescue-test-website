Our story

Jane, our founder, started Twin Cities Animal Rescue with the belief that every animal deserve a home. Since 2016, we've housed over 200 animals.

As a nonprofit, we rely on the community. This includes adopters, foster homes, volunteers, and donors.

Our core values

At Twin Cities Animal Rescue, we put compassion first. We treat every animal with patience and respect. We also believe in the strength of community. We started with one person, but with the help of dozens of volunteers, we have changed the lives of hundreds of animals.
