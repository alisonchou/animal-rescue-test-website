Our mission

Welcome to our website! Our mission is to help animals without a home by connecting them with people who want companionship.

Our impact

Over the past ten years, Twin Cities Animal Rescue has made a great impact!

  • 200+ animals homed each year
  • 30+ volunteers and staff
  • 70+ foster homes

How You Can Help

  • Adopt: Add a new member to your family!
  • Foster: Give an animal in need a temporary home.
  • Volunteer: Take care of our animals and help run our shelter!
  • Donate: Running a shelter is expensive! Every donation counts.
